About Platter

For the cook who also wants to sit down.

Dinner goes well when the cook is calm. The cook is calm when there is a plan.

What we do

Platter is a small independent site about getting dinner to the table. We do not publish hundreds of recipes. We publish a method: how to time a meal, how to scale it for the number you are feeding and how to cook through the week without starting from nothing every evening.

How we do it

Each guide is written from home cooking, checked against published food-safety guidance on cooking, cooling and reheating, and read by a second person before it appears. Timings are honest averages. Ovens and appetites differ, and we say so.

What we leave out

Nothing is sold here and nobody pays to be mentioned. We do not give medical or dietary advice, and we do not make promises about health. If something on the site is wrong, tell us through the contact page and it will be corrected.
